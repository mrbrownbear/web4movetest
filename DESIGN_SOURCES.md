## Original creative production

The lone physical neighbourhood is the new uploaded model. The site adds original atmospheric road contours, geometry based fallback renderings, scene travel, a reversible dusk palette, street point lighting, an audience stage, dynamic valuation illustration and responsive dark / light compositions.

All model derived fallback frames come from a headless VTK rendering of the supplied geometry. There is no previous model, no image of the old website and no stock neighbourhood scene in this archive.
